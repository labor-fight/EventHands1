The previous 51-D state is decoded by MANO forward kinematics; 778 vertices are point-projected with a depth buffer to obtain occupancy and inverse-depth maps. The nonzero support of the four-channel input defines A0, and four subsequent support levels are constructed by 2×2 cell occupancy. A 3×3 input adapter, coordinate channels, and a sparse ResNet18-L3 with active-site MaskedBN encode the input. Concatenating the active-site mean and the feature sum divided by the full final-grid area gives a 512-D representation, which is mapped to a 51-D correction. A previous-state MLP supplies an additional correction; the entire correction is gated to zero when the LNES is empty, then added to the previous state. The resulting state is fed directly to the next step. Root and finger rotations use additive axis-angle parameters. Training uses a masked dense implementation and inference uses active-site sparse execution. Sequence shape β and camera intrinsics K are fixed; this configuration uses sequence-ground-truth β. Mesh, event, and support drawings are illustrative rather than measured examples. No filtering or update deadband is included.
